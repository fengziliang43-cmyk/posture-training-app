A steady daily routine for your back, made for adults living with scoliosis

Zixu turns a two-minute check-in (how your back feels, your energy, the time you have) into a short, conservative exercise routine. If you report a warning sign, it tells you to stop for the day and see a doctor instead.

How it works

  1. Check in

    Rate your back discomfort from 0 to 10, and say how much energy you have and how many minutes you can spare.

  2. Get today's routine

    Zixu picks a few low-load exercises, such as dead bug, bird dog and glute bridge, gives each one clear stop rules, and fits the session to your day.

  3. Keep a record

    Note how your back feels afterwards, and take posture photos the same way each time so you can compare them side by side.

Safety comes first

Zixu won't build a routine on a day you report any of these:

  • Numbness or tingling in your legs
  • Pain that shoots down a leg
  • New weakness in a leg or foot
  • Changes in bladder or bowel control
  • Sudden, sharp back pain

These can be signs that something needs a doctor's attention. If your back feels clearly worse after a session, Zixu can make the next one lighter, or turn it into a rest day.

What Zixu does not do

  • It doesn't diagnose scoliosis or measure your curve.
  • It makes no promises about changing your curve.
  • It isn't a substitute for your doctor or physiotherapist. If you're under their care, follow their plan first.
  • It isn't for children or teenagers. If your child has scoliosis, please follow their specialist's advice.

Who it's for

Adults (18 and over) who have been told they have scoliosis and want a gentle, regular way to stay active and keep track of how they feel.
